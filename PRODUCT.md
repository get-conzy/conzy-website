# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Owners and managers of small-to-mid Turkish businesses — özel clinics/hospitals, beauty & aesthetic centers, restaurants, auto service shops, real estate offices, legal/consulting firms, education institutions, and e-commerce/boutiques. They're busy operators, not technical buyers: they want fewer missed messages and less manual back-and-forth, not a "platform" to learn. They land on this page from ads/referrals, are comparing against doing nothing (manual WhatsApp replies) or generic chatbot tools, and the job to be done is booking a demo call.

## Product Purpose

Qavio is a WhatsApp AI chatbot + WhatsApp Flow automation service: it detects intent in incoming messages, opens interactive in-app forms (appointment booking, quotes, intake) without ever sending the customer to an external link, and gives the business owner a live dashboard of conversations, bookings, and stats. Qavio also handles the Meta Business API setup end-to-end. Success = visitor requests a demo via the contact form; the page must build enough trust and clarity in one scroll to get a busy, non-technical owner to do that.

## Brand Personality

Premium & approachable. Confident, capable automation — but it should feel like a helpful person who gets your business, not a faceless enterprise platform or a hyped-up startup. Warm without being cutesy; polished without being corporate-stiff. The existing visual language (deep forest-green hero, single orange action color, Cormorant Garamond display serif against Inter body text, warm beige page background, a playful rounded "Bagel Fat One" logotype) already carries this tone — lean into it rather than replacing it.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI-SaaS template aesthetic: cream/sand near-white body backgrounds, gradient-text headlines, tiny uppercase tracked "eyebrow" labels above every section, numbered 01/02/03 scaffolding on non-sequential content, identical icon+heading+text card grids.
- Corporate/enterprise stiffness — overly formal tone, navy-and-grey palettes, dense jargon. The audience is a salon or clinic owner, not an IT department.

## Design Principles

- **Show, don't tell.** The live WhatsApp/Flow/dashboard phone mockup is the core proof — features should be demonstrated in motion wherever possible, not just listed as bullet claims.
- **Stay inside WhatsApp.** The product's differentiator is "no external links, no app switching." Visuals and copy should keep reinforcing that the entire experience lives inside the chat the customer already has open.
- **Speak the owner's industry.** Sector-specific framing (klinik, restoran, güzellik, oto servis, emlak, etc.) beats generic "AI assistant for businesses" messaging — specificity builds trust with this audience.
- **Calm confidence over hype.** Premium and trustworthy, not loud/startup-hustle, and not corporate-cold either. One accent color (orange) used deliberately, not scattered.
- **Bilingual by design.** Turkish-first, with a fully equivalent English translation via the i18n system. Layout, type scale, and component widths must hold up in both languages — Turkish strings often run longer.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard WCAG AA: body text and placeholders need ≥4.5:1 contrast (watch the warm beige/cream surfaces against `--text2`/`--text3` grays), large/display text ≥3:1, visible focus states on all interactive elements (custom cursor must not hide native focus rings for keyboard users), and a `prefers-reduced-motion` fallback for the custom cursor, marquee, hero phone-screen animations, and scroll-reveal (`.fu`) effects.
